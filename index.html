Report Builder — v2.6 · Ads Manager template

Built around a real Ads Manager export: weekly breakdown, Amount spent in the export's currency, Results split by what they actually are. Weekly rows are aggregated by ad before anything is ranked, and totals are campaign to date as exported — not a 7-day slice. Two charts are included: spend by ad set, and a scatter of every ad by impressions, spend and CTR. Every report opens with a TLDR top 3, and exports as HTML, PDF, or a Sheets-ready CSV.

Template: v2.6 — locked  ·  Nothing is sent anywhere — the file is read in your browser only.  ·  Flow: upload CSV → check → print / save as PDF. Nothing auto-sends.

1 · Upload your CSV

Your Ads Manager export as-is — the columns it already has. A weekly breakdown is fine: one row per ad per week is aggregated by ad, so the same ad doesn't appear three times in the top 5. Works with: Reporting starts / ends, Ad name, Campaign / Ad set name, Results + Result indicator, Reach, Amount spent, Impressions, Link clicks. Currency is read from the Amount spent header (USD, SGD…). Extra columns are ignored. Quoted names with commas are handled.

QA — before it goes out

Reach is not additive: the same people appear in multiple ads and weeks, so the Reach tile is a sum with that caveat, not a deduplicated unique count. Results are split by Result indicator below the tiles whenever they mix different actions.
